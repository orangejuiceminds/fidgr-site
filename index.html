Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

fidgr is a one-minute mental reset app made by Orangejuiceminds. This page explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your data.

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • There are no ads, and the app does not use your advertising ID.
  • Your reset history and settings stay on your phone.
  • The app sends a small amount of anonymous usage and crash information to help us fix problems and understand which resets people use.

What stays on your device

The app stores the following on your phone only. We do not receive it.

  • Which resets you completed, when, and for how long.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, theme, reduced motion, and whether you have finished onboarding.
  • Your best score in the Click toy.

You can erase all of this at any time from the Privacy & Data screen in the app. Uninstalling the app also removes it. The app opts out of Android's cloud backup, so this data is not copied to your Google account by the app.

What the app sends to us

Anonymous usage events. We use an analytics service (PostHog) to count a few simple events. The only events the app can send are:

  • that onboarding was completed, and whether it was skipped;
  • that a reset started or finished, which reset it was, and how long it lasted;
  • that an audio file failed to play;
  • that feedback was submitted, and its category (not what you wrote);
  • that someone tapped interest in fidgr pro.

These events contain no names, email addresses, free text, location or contacts. The analytics service may assign a random anonymous identifier to the installation so it can count events, and may receive technical details such as the app version and device type. We do not link this to who you are.

Crash reports. If the app crashes or hits an error, a report is sent to an error-monitoring service (Sentry). Such reports can include technical information such as the error details, app version, phone model and Android version, and your approximate network address as seen by the service. They do not include your reset history or settings.

What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It does not use your advertising ID or track you across other apps or websites.
  • It does not sell your data or share it for advertising.

The only Android permissions it uses are for playing audio (changing audio settings and a media-playback foreground service).

Feedback

The feedback screen prepares a message (your chosen category, any note you type, the app version and your phone model) and opens your phone's share sheet. You choose where to send it, for example by email. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Service providers

We use PostHog for anonymous usage events and Sentry for crash reports. They process that information on our behalf under their own privacy terms. We do not give them anything else.

Children

fidgr is a general-audience wellbeing app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

Because we hold no account or personal profile for you, there is nothing to look up or delete on our side. You can erase everything stored on your phone from inside the app. If you have a question or want to ask about data, write to us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
